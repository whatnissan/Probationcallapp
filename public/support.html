Support

ProbationCall calls your county's probation drug-test hotline every morning, listens to the announcement, and tells you by push, text, or email whether you have to report for testing today.

Contact us

Email: probationreportingapp@gmail.com

Tell us the email address on your account and, if it is about a specific morning, the date. We answer every message.

Please remember

ProbationCall is a convenience, not a substitute for calling your hotline. Calls fail, recordings are unclear, and speech recognition makes mistakes. If you are ever unsure, call the hotline yourself. You are responsible for your own reporting.
